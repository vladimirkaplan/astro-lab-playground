PHY110 lab practice

Physics lab benches for PHY110. Each lab has three parts. You get a short talk, a bench you can play with, and exercises that mark themselves. Start at Lab 1 and work down the list. Every lab also has a worksheet. Print it, or save it as .csv or .xlsx and fill it in on paper.

This is for practice and understanding only. It is not how you hand in a lab. Do the real lab, and follow your instructor's rules.

Practice sim, not your BMCC submission. Explanations are written for this site. Topic order follows OpenStax College Physics 2e for further reading.

Labs

A green tick means you passed every exercise on that lab's Check tab. Ticks stay in this browser only. The cards follow the lab order in the course manual.

Every bench prints the value it grades you against. Your own number is allowed to come out wrong, and the page says why.

Pick Ideal or Fun on each bench. Ideal reads the instrument exactly. Fun adds the noise a real instrument has, and it never moves an accepted value.

Fourteen labs are open now. The rest of the course will appear here in course order as each one is built.

Lab 1✔ Start here

Units, instruments, and percent error

Measure the shoebox on both rulers, then work out your own percent error.

 Lab 2✔

Graph scales, plotting, and slope

Plot five points, draw one trendline, and pull the slope out of it.

 Lab 3✔

Free fall: measuring g

Time a falling ball, average five drops, and compare your g with 9.80.

 Lab 4✔

Atwood’s machine: Newton’s second law

Build an unbalanced pair of hangers, time five drops, and pull a out of your average.

 Lab 5✔

Spring constant and vertical spring periods

Measure K from a loaded spring, time ten cycles, and check your period.

 Lab 6✔

Torque balance on a meter stick

Set a meter stick level with hanging masses, then weigh an unknown object.

 Lab 7✔

Buoyant force and Archimedes’ principle

Measure the displaced water and the lost weight, and check the buoyant force.

 Lab 8✔

Specific heat of a metal by mixtures

Find a metal’s specific heat by dropping it from boiling water into a cup.

 Lab 9✔

Ohm’s law from a V-I line

Set the supply to six voltages, log the current, and pull the resistance out of the V-I slope.

 Lab 10✔

Series and parallel resistor rules

Build a series pair and a parallel pair, then log the meters to test the rules.

 Lab 11✔

Speed of sound from a resonance tube

Find two resonance positions for each fork and pull the speed of sound out of the spacing.

 Lab 12✔

Reflection, refraction, and the critical angle

Trace the laser at a water surface, log the bend, and pull the index from the critical angle.

 Lab 13✔

Converging lens focal length and a two-lens refractor

Find the two sharp spots, and pull both focal lengths out of the bench.

 Lab 14✔

Line and continuous spectra with a spectroscope

Match the lamp lines to the scale, and name each gas by its fingerprint.
