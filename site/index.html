Astro Lab Playground

Each intro astronomy lab here has three parts. You get a short talk, a sim you can play with, and exercises that mark themselves. The labs follow the BMCC AST110 order, but any first course can use them. It is free, it needs no account, it runs with no wifi, and nothing leaves your device. Each lab also has a worksheet. Print it, or save it as .csv or .xlsx and hand it in on paper.

This is for practice and understanding only. It is not how you hand in a lab. Do the real lab, and follow your instructor's rules.

Practice sim, not your BMCC submission. This site explains the physics and lets you practice the measurements. The words are based on OpenStax Astronomy 2e (CC BY 4.0); spectral line data cites NIST.

Labs

A green tick means you passed every exercise on that lab's Check tab. Ticks stay in this browser only. The cards follow the lab order in the course manual.

Lab 1✔ Start here

Measurement

Measure with the on-screen ruler and coin balance, then work out percent error.

 Lab 2✔

Mathematics for astronomy

Measure pi with a tape, then trade angle for size with the number 206,265.

 Lab 3✔

The celestial sphere

Find any star by its RA and Dec, then find the Sun on your birthday.

 Lab 4✔

Phases of the Moon

Spin the Sun-Earth-Moon angle and watch the phase, moonrise, and moonset change.

 Lab 5✔

Moonrise and eclipses

Tilt the two sky roads on one board and see when eclipses can happen.

 Lab 6✔

Retrograde motion of Mars

Drive Earth past Mars, trace the backward loop, then log 28 nights of motion.

 Lab 7✔

Lenses and telescopes

Chase the sharpest image on the ray bench, then build a small spyglass.

 Lab 8✔

Phases of Venus

Orbit Venus and watch its phase, stretch from the Sun, and size all change.

 Lab 9✔

Acceleration of gravity

Time 15 swings, fit T^2 against length, and work out 9.8 m/s^2 by hand.

 Lab 10✔

Spectra

Read the bright lines of real gases, then match each unknown to its gas.

 Lab 11✔

Heliocentric parallax

Watch near stars draw yearly ellipses, then measure a hidden star's distance.

 Lab 12✔

The H-R diagram

Plot 29 real stars by heat and light, then work out how wide each one is.

 Lab 13✔

Variable stars and Cepheids

Fold a light curve until the period pops out, then turn it into a distance.

 Lab 14✔

Galaxy identification

Sort ten real galaxy photos along the tuning fork, then name them from memory.

 Lab 15✔

Hubble's Law

Measure galaxies on a swelling balloon, fit the slope, and get an age.

 Labs 16-17✔

Time, latitude, longitude

Read any place in degrees, then set any clock from the turning sky.

Work without internet

Every file here is static and uses relative links, so it runs with no connection. Download the whole site as one zip, unzip it into any folder, and open index.html. Any static server works too. On the live site your browser keeps the pages after your first visit, so they reload offline. On a school LAN mirror, just unzip and serve the folder. Nothing on the site calls the network, so plain http works.
