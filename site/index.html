Astro Lab Playground

Every intro-astronomy lab as a short explanation, an imitation you can play with, and self-graded exercises. Built for the BMCC AST110 lab sequence, useful for any first-course student. Free, offline-friendly, no account, nothing leaves your device.

Practice and understanding only. It is not a lab submission tool: do the real lab, follow your instructor's rules.

Practice sim, not your BMCC submission. This site explains the physics and lets you rehearse the measurements. Written material is adapted from OpenStax Astronomy 2e (CC BY 4.0); spectral line data cites NIST.

Labs

Cards mark a green tick when you pass every exercise on a lab's Check tab. Ticks are saved in this browser only.

Lab 9✔

Spectra

Bench spectroscope with real gas lines. Read fingerprints, match unknowns.

 Lab 15✔

Hubble's Law

Galaxies on an inflating balloon. Measure velocities, fit the slope, get an age.

 Lab 1✔

Measurement

On-screen ruler and coin balance: parallax, conversions, percent error.

 Lab 8✔

Acceleration of gravity

Pendulum bench: time 15 swings, fit T^2 vs L, squeeze 9.8 m/s^2 out of a stopwatch.

 Lab 7✔

Lenses and telescopes

Ray bench: chase the sharpest card spot, learn 1/f = 1/d_o + 1/d_i, build a spyglass.

 Lab 5✔

Phases of the Moon

Spin the Sun-Earth-Moon angle; real NY rise/set times computed on your device.

 Lab 12✔

Moonrise and eclipses

Node lines and season wedges on a Sun-locked board; the real 2027 NASA events table, tap to visit.

 Labs 16-17✔

Time, latitude, longitude

Graticule dropper and clock-face zones: read any address in degrees, wind any clock.

 Lab 3✔

The celestial sphere

Pan-and-zoom RA/Dec chart with the ecliptic, birthday-Sun finder, star quiz.

 Venus✔

Phases of Venus

Orbit viewer: phase, elongation and apparent size together, plus what Ptolemy predicted. Galileo's killing evidence.

 Lab 2✔

Mathematics for astronomy

Measure pi with a tape, trade angle for size with 206,265, and watch the Moon and Sun swap diameters.

 HR✔

The H-R diagram

29 real stars plotted by temperature and luminosity; click one, then derive its radius and see why giants are huge and white dwarfs are Earth-sized.

 Parallax✔

Heliocentric parallax

Watch nearby stars trace their yearly ellipses, squash the ellipse flat with the latitude slider, then be Bessel: measure a hidden star and get its distance.

 Vars✔

Variable stars and Cepheids

Fold light curves until the period snaps out of the blob, then be Leavitt: reduce a hidden cluster Cepheid from period to distance by hand.

Homework practice

Self-check benches that rehearse the homework method with freshly generated numbers each attempt. These are practice only - not a submission channel, and they never compute your graded assignment's blanks. Ticks save in this browser when you pass every question on a page's Check tab.

HW 1✔

Homework 1 practice

Stars, galaxies, units and scientific notation: concept checks, unit-factor chains, light-travel distances, and sci-notation with the micro/milli trap - all auto-graded, all fresh numbers.

 HW 2✔

Homework 2 practice

Newton's gravitation and second-law ratios: forward and reverse ratio tables, a live-ratio bench, and the notation-invariance idea - graded against the same solvers the tables use.
